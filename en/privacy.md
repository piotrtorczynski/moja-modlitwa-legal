# Privacy Policy

Effective: 10 October 2026

This policy describes what data the **Moja Modlitwa** app for iOS and Android (the “App”) processes and why. It is a translation of the [Polish version](../../privacy/); if the two differ, the Polish version prevails.

## Data controller

The data controller is Piotr Torczynski, the author of the App. Contact: [mojamodlitwa.app@gmail.com](mailto:mojamodlitwa.app@gmail.com).

## What stays on your phone

The App does not require an account or sign-in. What you save in it is stored only on your device:

- prayer intentions,
- novena and rosary progress,
- formation commitments and meetings (Domestic Church, Equipes Notre-Dame),
- favourite prayers, highlights in Holy Scripture and lectio divina notes,
- settings, including reminder times.

We have no access to this data. Reminders are scheduled on the phone and do not pass through our server. If you have system backup turned on (iCloud or Google), this data may be included in it under Apple’s or Google’s terms. Deleting the App removes it from the device.

## What goes to the internet

**Liturgical readings.** The App downloads the readings of the day from Evangelizo (feed.evangelizo.org). The request contains only the date and the language. Like any internet connection, it reveals your IP address to the server. Evangelizo processes it under its own privacy policy.

**Statistics and crash reports (iOS only).** The iOS version uses Google Firebase services:

- **Firebase Analytics** – information about which features you use, e.g. screens opened, novenas or rosaries started and finished, prayers viewed, the type of formation chosen, the category of an added intention (without its content), appearance settings. Plus technical data: a random installation identifier, device model, system and app version, approximate country or region derived from the IP address.
- **Firebase Crashlytics** – crash reports: the state of the App at the time of the error, device model, system and app version, a random installation identifier.

This data does not include your name, e-mail address, or the content of your intentions and notes. It is used only to improve the App and to see which features are used. We do not use it for advertising and do not sell it. We keep analytics data for no longer than 14 months and crash reports for 90 days. Google may process this data outside the European Economic Area on the basis of standard contractual clauses. Details: [Firebase – Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

The Android version currently sends no statistics or crash reports. If this changes, we will update this policy before releasing such a version.

## Messages to us

In Profile you can write to us, suggest a change or report a bug. The App then prepares a message in your e-mail app and sends nothing by itself. Below the space for your text it adds the app version, system version, phone model, language, the chosen formation (DK, END or none) and which option you are writing from. You see this information before sending and can delete it. The message, together with your e-mail address, goes to the mojamodlitwa.app@gmail.com mailbox in Gmail (Google). We use it only to reply and to improve the App, and delete it once the matter is resolved.

## Legal basis

We process analytics data and crash reports on the basis of our legitimate interest in maintaining and developing the App (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We process the messages you send us in order to reply to them (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

## Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to rectify, erase and port it, to restrict its processing, and to object. Because analytics data is not linked to your identity, please say in your message what your request concerns, and we will do our best to help. You may also lodge a complaint with the President of the Personal Data Protection Office in Poland (uodo.gov.pl).

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We will announce significant changes to this policy in the App or in the update notes. The history of changes is publicly available in the [repository of this document](https://github.com/piotrtorczynski/moja-modlitwa-legal/commits/main/en/privacy.md).
